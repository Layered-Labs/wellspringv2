04 · Timing

When to bank

Banking works best from clear speech, before treatment changes your voice.

  • Before laryngectomy / esophageal surgeryBank now
  • Early brain tumour / ALS diagnosisBank early
  • Voice already changingBank what you can + family clips
  • Already using AACReplace generic voice

Ask your speech-language pathologist (SLP) or surgical team to share this page at diagnosis — that timing matters more than recording quality. Six short sentences in a quiet room beat an hour of tired recording.

Consent & safety: Only bank your own voice, or a loved one's with their clear permission. A voice is biometric data. This app has no accounts and no sharing — a clone can only be played on this device, in this browser, unless you export your own clips.

A note on honesty: Loading a 0.6B model in a phone browser depends on WebGPU and device memory. On devices where Qwen can't load yet, this page automatically falls back — first to Chatterbox via the voxshot pattern, then to your device's system voice tuned with the pitch from your recordings — so you can always type and be heard today, and get the full Qwen clone on a WebGPU-capable browser (Chrome / Edge on desktop & recent Android).
